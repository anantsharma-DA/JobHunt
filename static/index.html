Your resume details

JobHunt uses these to write a resume for each job. They stay on this computer; only these details and the job's description are sent to the AI service you choose on the Settings tab, and only when you press Tailor resume on a job. The AI may only reword what is here: it is never allowed to invent employers, dates, numbers or skills, and anything new is flagged for you before it reaches the PDF.

Interview questions

Pick one or more job titles. JobHunt searches the web for questions real candidates reported being asked for that role, and shows where each one was found. Questions reported on 2 or more different sites are marked verified and listed first. Answers come from the source page when it gives one; otherwise the AI drafts one, clearly labelled. Everything found is kept on the Saved Questions tab. Searching a job title again looks for new questions only: it uses different search wording, skips pages already read, and shows just what wasn't saved yet ("0 new questions" if there were none). For one company's questions, use Frequently Asked Questions on a job.

CTC to monthly in-hand pay

New tax regime, FY 2026-27: ₹75,000 standard deduction, no tax up to ₹12 lakh of taxable income (section 87A rebate, with marginal relief just above), then the new-regime slabs, surcharge above ₹50 lakh and 4% cess. Match the options to your offer letter.

Warnings

Signs shown on job cards, worked out on this computer (no AI, no cost). They are signs, not proof: each badge lists its reasons. A scam warning looks for fees or deposits, WhatsApp/Telegram-only or Gmail recruiters, "no interview" offers, easy-money tasks and pay far above the experience asked. A possible ghost job has stayed up for a long time or keeps being reposted, often without real hiring behind it.

Reminders and daily auto-search

The Applied tab reminds you to follow up and warns you before interviews. With the daily auto-search on, JobHunt searches your saved job titles, cities and sites for jobs posted in the last day at the time you choose, then tells you how many are new and how many are strong matches. Notifications appear in the corner of your screen (Windows notifications) while JobHunt is running.

If JobHunt isn't open then, Windows starts it in a minimised window; it searches, shows the notification, then closes. Only for your Windows user; no administrator rights. Untick to remove it.

AI for resume tailoring

Needed only for the AI features: Tailor resume, cover notes, referral messages, practice feedback, drafted interview answers, Check with AI and reading an uploaded resume. Searching for jobs works without it. OpenRouter, NVIDIA and Gemini have free options. Add a key for as many services as you like: they are tried from the top down, so if the first is busy, out of credit or down, the next one takes over. Use the arrows to change the order. Each key is kept on this computer and is sent only to its own service.

Web search for interview questions

Used by Frequently Asked Questions on each job and by the Interview Questions tab. A Tavily key gives 1,000 free credits a month, and a search uses 2 to 4 of them because JobHunt also reads the pages it finds (get one at app.tavily.com). The Gemini key on its card above is tried first, but Google only lets older keys, or keys with billing switched on, search for free; on newer free keys JobHunt notices and uses Tavily instead. JobHunt counts both and never goes past the free limits.

Good to know

Searching

  • Naukri blocks automated requests, so Naukri searches and Update Applicants use a Microsoft Edge window placed off-screen. You may see Edge in the taskbar; don't close it.
  • LinkedIn limits how many searches it allows. If it shows "blocked", wait 30–60 minutes and search again.
  • Companies with their own careers site (not Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters or Workday) are covered only through Indeed or LinkedIn. For a long list, choose "Indeed only" above.
  • If a site stops working, update the site reader: .venv\Scripts\python -m pip install -U --no-deps python-jobspy

Warnings and Insights

  • Scam and possible ghost job badges are signs, not proof. Check with AI in Details gives a second opinion.
  • Salary ranges come only from jobs that show pay, and the in-hand pay is an estimate (new tax regime, FY 2026-27). Check the offer letter and payslip.

AI and keys

  • Searching needs no key. The AI features (tailoring, cover notes, referral messages, practice feedback, drafted answers) need one AI key; free ones work. Interview questions need a Tavily key or a Gemini key.
  • Everything the AI writes is checked against your Resume tab; flagged words aren't in your details.
  • Keys are encrypted for your Windows account. After moving JobHunt to another computer, paste them again.
  • Tavily gives 1,000 free credits a month; opening saved questions costs nothing.

Running

  • JobHunt, its reminders and the daily auto-search run only while the run.bat window is open, unless Start JobHunt automatically is ticked above.
  • Windows notifications appear under "Windows PowerShell".

Your data

  • Everything is kept in the data folder inside JobHunt (tailored PDFs in data\resumes), never uploaded.
  • To update or move JobHunt, copy the data folder into the new copy.
