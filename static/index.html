Your resume details

JobHunt uses these to write a resume for each job. They stay on this computer; only these details and the job's description are sent to the AI service you choose on the Settings tab, and only when you press Tailor resume on a job. Interview questions

Pick one or more job titles. JobHunt searches the web for questions real candidates reported being asked for that role, and shows where each one was found. Questions reported on 2 or more different sites are marked verified and listed first. Answers come from the source page when it gives one; otherwise the AI drafts one, clearly labelled. Everything found is kept on the Saved Questions tab. Searching a job title again looks for new questions only: it uses different search wording, skips pages already read, and shows just what wasn't saved yet ("0 new questions" if there were none). For one company's questions, use Frequently Asked Questions on a job.

Good to know

  • Naukri blocks automated requests, so Naukri searches run in a Microsoft Edge window placed off-screen. You may see Edge in the taskbar during a search; don't close it.
  • LinkedIn limits how many searches it allows. If it shows "blocked", wait 30–60 minutes and search again.
  • Job sites change their pages from time to time. If a source stops working, update JobSpy: .venv\Scripts\python -m pip install -U --no-deps python-jobspy
  • Companies with their own custom careers sites are only covered as far as they post jobs on Indeed or LinkedIn. For a long company list, choose "Indeed only" above so LinkedIn doesn't block your main search.
  • The app runs only while the run.bat window is open.
